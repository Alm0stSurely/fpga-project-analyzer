NEORV32 RISC-V SoC: diagram and live RTL simulation

Left: the FPGA Project Analyzer (fpa) diagram of unmodified NEORV32 v1.13.7 RTL (neorv32_test_setup_bootloader with the Arty A7 constraints). Right: the same RTL running live in your browser, converted GHDL → Verilog → Verilator → WebAssembly and clocked at a real-time 1 MHz. Blocks glow while the simulated SoC uses them; click a glowing block to jump to its widget, double-click to open it.
